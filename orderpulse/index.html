OrderPulse

Parcel bhejne se pehle customer se pooch lein

COD order courier ko dene se pehle OrderPulse customer se WhatsApp par tasdeeq leta hai. Jis ne mana kar diya, wo parcel nikalta hi nahin.

Ek order khud chala kar dekhein

Ye namuna order hai. Dekhein aap ke customer ko kya message milega aur aap ko kya nazar aayega.

    Aap ko kya milega

    Khud-kaar WhatsApp

    Har status par customer ko message, Urdu ya English mein. Message ka matn aap badal sakte hain.

    Bhejne se pehle tasdeeq

    Haan, ji, nahi, cancel, Urdu ya Roman Urdu, jawab khud samajh liya jata hai. Saaf na ho to aap ko bataya jata hai.

    Apna tracking page

    Har order ka link, jis par status, rider aur delivery rating ho.

    Wapsi ki wajah

    Dashboard dikhata hai parcel kyun wapas aaya: number band, ghalat pata, customer ka inkar, ya jaali order.

    Shuru karne ke liye kya chahiye

    • Aap ka store: Shopify, WooCommerce ya apna panel.
    • WhatsApp Business number: Twilio ke zariye, customer ko messages isi se jayenge.
    • Meta ki manzoori: pehle se bheje janay wale message templates ke liye.

    Abhi ki haalat: poora nizam demo mode mein test ho chuka hai. Asli WhatsApp number par pehla test pilot mein hoga, is liye shuru mein thore orders par chalayenge.

    Kya ye aap ke store ke liye theek hai?

    Behtareen. Jis ne aap ko ye page bheja hai, usay ye message bhej dein:

    OrderPulse pilot chahiye. Mera store: ____ , rozana orders: ____

    Koi baat nahi, shukriya ke aap ne dekha. Jab COD wapsi masla bane, ye page dobara khol lein.
